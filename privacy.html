Privacy Policy

Nàdar

Last updated: 26 September 2026

The short version

Nàdar collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code of any kind. Your notebook is kept on your own devices and, if you are signed in to iCloud and leave sync on, in your own private iCloud database. The developer cannot see any of it.

What the app stores, and where

The wildlife entries, photographs, references and simulations are part of the app itself.

Everything you put in the Field Notebook — text, dates, an optional location, photographs, video, sound recordings and web links — is stored on your device.

If iCloud sync is on, the same notes are also stored in your own private CloudKit database, which is part of your personal iCloud account. Apple provides that storage; the developer has no access to it and no way to obtain access. It is not a shared or public database, and nothing is stored on any server belonging to the developer, because there is no such server.

You can turn sync off at the bottom of the Notebook tab. Deleting a note deletes it from every device.

What is sent over the network

Only iCloud sync, and only if it is on. The app makes no other network requests of its own: no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and the wildlife content and simulations are inside the app so they work without a connection. When you tap a web link, it opens in your browser or in Safari, not inside code of ours.

Permissions the app may ask for, and why

  • Photo library — so you can add photographs and video to a note. The app receives only what you choose.
  • Camera (iPhone and iPad) — only when you tap Camera, to take a photo or video for a note.
  • Microphone — only when you tap Record, to make a sound recording for a note.
  • Location — only when you save a note with Stamp my location switched on, to record where you were. It is never read in the background, and never sent to us.

Declining any of these leaves the rest of the app working normally.

Photographs

The wildlife photographs in the app come from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons licences. The photographer and licence are shown under each photograph.

Children

Nàdar has no accounts, no messaging, no user-generated content shared with anyone, and no data collection. It gathers nothing from anybody, of any age.

Your data is yours

Any photograph, video or recording in a note can be opened and shared or saved from its viewer, and the text of a note or of the whole notebook can be shared or copied. Nothing about your notes needs this app to read them. Photographs you add are copies; your originals are never changed.

Deleting the app deletes its local data. Notes synced to iCloud can be removed in Settings under your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
