A field companion to British wildlife, month by month.

What to look for in the British countryside, month by month. For every month of the year: the mammals, birds, amphibians, reptiles and fish, invertebrates, plants and fungi to look out for, with what each is doing, some natural history and folklore, page references to field guides, and places worth visiting.

There are interactive simulations — how spring has moved across Europe, where British birds migrate, and how flowering plants reproduce — and a field notebook for your own sightings, with photos, video, sound recordings and links, that syncs between your devices through your own iCloud.

No accounts, no adverts, no tracking. Free.
