Common questions

What is this app?

A month-by-month companion to British wildlife: what to look out for each month, with natural history, folklore, page references to field guides, places to visit, some interactive simulations and a field notebook.

Where is my notebook kept?

On your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud and sync is on, in your own private iCloud, so it appears on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere else. See the Privacy Policy.

My notebook is not syncing.

The line at the bottom of the Notebook tab says whether it is syncing and, if not, why — usually no iCloud account is signed in, iCloud Drive is off, the device is offline, or iCloud storage is full. Your notes are safe on the device meanwhile. Syncing is not instant; allow a minute. Both devices need the same Apple ID.

Can I keep my notebook on one device only?

Yes. Turn off Sync with iCloud at the bottom of the Notebook tab and restart the app.

What do the page numbers mean?

Many entries say where to find that species in field guides, so you can look it up in your own copy. The guides are not included in the app, and page numbers depend on the edition. If you spot a wrong page number, please tell me.

Can I fold sections away?

Yes. Tap a heading such as Birds, on the month pages or in Learn More, to close or open it.

The photograph shows the wrong species.

Please tell me which entry and month. Where a photograph shows a close relative, a note under the photograph usually says so.

The camera, microphone or location is not working.

Check the permission: on iPhone and iPad, Settings → Nàdar; on a Mac, System Settings → Privacy & Security. The rest of the app works without them.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The wildlife entries, photographs and simulations are inside the app. Only iCloud sync and web links need a connection.
